=== GROUND ===
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production pixel-art animation sprite sheet for a small desktop AI companion.
Input image 1 is the exact character identity and pixel-art style reference, a phone photo of a tiny Spider-Man hanging upside down. Reconstruct the sprite cleanly, do NOT reproduce the photographed monitor, glare, grain or background.
Create ONE square transparent PNG sprite sheet, exactly 6 columns by 6 rows, 36 individual sprites. Equal square cells, perfect regular grid. No labels, no lettering, no grid lines, no checkerboard baked into the art.
Character identity: tiny extremely chunky retro 8-bit chibi Spider-Man, disproportionately large rounded red head, ONE very large white eye with a charcoal border visible in RIGHT-FACING PROFILE, tiny red torso, simple cyan-blue limbs and suit accents, red boots/gloves, dark charcoal outlines. The huge mask/head accounts for about 55% of total standing height, matching the supplied upside-down reference. Use only about 6 flat colors: vivid red, darker red accent, cyan-blue, dark blue accent, charcoal, off-white. No detailed web lattice on mask, no realistic anatomy, no glossy highlights. Think approximately 32x40 logical pixel character enlarged with nearest-neighbor, clean chunky square pixels, consistent thickness and scale.
Composition: output ideally 1536x1536; each of 36 equal cells contains exactly one full-body character centered horizontally. Character around 150 pixels high within each 256 pixel cell, generous transparent gutters. All frames are entirely inside their cell. Ground contact at the same local y about 210 for grounded rows. All face RIGHT, side view. Camera and head size stay completely fixed across frames. Successive columns are meaningful successive animation frames, NOT unrelated poses.
Rows, top to bottom, exactly:
1 IDLE breathing loop: neutral, slight inhale, upper inhale, slight exhale, neutral, gentle blink. Very subtle body bounce, both feet on the same floor line.
2 WALK RIGHT loop: 6 distinct gait frames, opposing arms/legs; contact right foot, down, passing, contact left foot, down, passing. Character remains in place within cell, motion will be in software.
3 RUN RIGHT loop: 6 distinct exaggerated running gait frames, forward lean, alternating long strides and tucked airborne passing poses. Still tiny oversized same head.
4 JUMP takeoff: upright ready, deep crouch, arms preparing, pushing off, airborne extending, airborne tucked. Keep same fixed camera and logical origin; within cell only.
5 FALL loop: 6 poses airborne with arms raised or spread, legs below, subtle alternating wind motion. No motion blur or particles.
6 LAND recovery: airborne near ground, one foot touches, deep landing squat, superhero low crouch, rising, standing neutral. Both full body and hands inside cell.
Background genuinely transparent alpha. No props, text, icons, floor shadows or particles. No duplicate extra characters. This is a raw animation source atlas, not a presentation poster.
